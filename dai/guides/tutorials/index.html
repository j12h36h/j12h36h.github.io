Recommended Learning Path

  1. Damage IndicatorsLearn what an ADDON is and how one event becomes one visible result.
  2. Comic Effects or Survival InterfaceGet comfortable with presentation and conditions.
  3. Echo Time or Fun SurvivalAdd state, input, action composition and reusable behavior.
  4. TamacraftiBuild your first persistent MAIN experience.
  5. ClayGrounds or MineTriggerMove into custom entities or combat systems.
  6. Hollow Spiral / AutoCraft MineShaft / MusashiCombine multiple subsystems into a game with a strong identity.

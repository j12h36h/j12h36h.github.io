Build Your Own Minecraft Automation

Decisions and Impulses (DAI) is a Minecraft automation framework designed to let datapack creators build their own gameplay behaviors without rewriting the mod itself.

DAI = EngineReusable player capabilities and runtime systems.
Your Datapack = GameplayYou decide what the player should do and when.
Endlessly ComposableSurvival, Creative, Adventure, maps, challenges, and more.

What DAI Provides

Instead of shipping one fixed way to play Minecraft, DAI provides reusable systems that third-party datapacks can combine into their own behaviors.

World Interaction

Movement, navigation, camera control, mining, placement, item collection, inventory interaction, and crafting.

Perception

Block detection, entity detection, structural recognition, conditions, and contextual world/player state.

Decision Systems

Objectives, sequences, conditions, menus, dynamic Available menus, fallbacks, and action routing.

Gameplay Systems

Exploration, combat, resource gathering, Creative construction, and higher-level behavior supplied by datapacks.

What Can You Make?

A datapack can contain one small behavior or an entire Minecraft progression system.

Survival Packs

Progression, tools, food, shelter, mining strategies, resource priorities, combat logic, or speedrun routes.

Creative Builders

Structures physically built by the player through Creative inventory, flight, camera movement, and block placement.

Adventure Maps

Landmarks, scripted routes, quests, interactions, target enemies, collection objectives, and world-reactive behavior.

Challenge Modes

Skyblock, hardcore strategies, biome restrictions, pacifist runs, one-block challenges, or entirely custom rules.

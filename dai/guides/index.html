Creator Guides

Build anything from one reusable addon to a complete branded Minecraft game. DAI Engine 2.2 keeps the broad JSON runtime and adds first-class authoritative dispatch for registered projectiles, particles, effects and potions while clarifying the boundary between general action/condition registries and native entity-behavior vocabulary.

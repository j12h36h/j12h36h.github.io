Privacy Policy

Last updated August 27, 2026.

What the service uses

If Google sign-in is enabled, Firebase Authentication provides the account identity used by the service. The application may display and store the account identifier, display name, profile image URL, and the content you intentionally publish.

Public content

Ideas, problems, projects, and posts created in public spaces are designed to be readable by other visitors. Do not publish private or sensitive information in public content.

Passwords

The service does not receive or store your Google password. Google handles the authentication flow.

Service data

When the Firebase backend is configured, published content is stored in Cloud Firestore. When the backend is not configured, the site operates in local demonstration mode and does not create a shared account.

Deletion

The initial release is designed so that authorship is tied to the authenticated Firebase user ID. Account/content deletion controls should be added before operating the service as a production community at scale.

Third-party services

Authentication and database functionality may use Google Firebase services and are therefore also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Contact

The site owner should replace this section with a public support contact before production OAuth verification.

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 28, 2026.

Google sign-in

If Google sign-in is enabled, Firebase Authentication receives the Google account information needed to authenticate you. LCS does not receive or store your Google password.

Your public LCS identity is separate

Your public LCS profile uses a display name that you choose. It does not need to match your Google account name or legal name. LCS does not copy your Google email address into your public profile or public posts. Your Google account name and email may be shown to you on your own Account page so you can confirm which account is signed in, but the LCS frontend does not write those values into the public profile document.

Public profile information

The public profile may store your Firebase user identifier, your chosen display name, an optional public bio, and a profile image URL only when you explicitly enable use of your Google profile picture. Google-profile-picture sharing is off by default.

Public content

Ideas, problems, projects, and posts created in public spaces are designed to be readable by other visitors. Public content is associated with your LCS public identity. Do not publish private or sensitive information in public content.

Profile changes

When you change your public display name or public profile picture setting, LCS attempts to update your existing authored posts and project objects so older content uses the same public identity. Your Google account itself is not renamed.

Service data

When the Firebase backend is configured, published content and public profiles are stored in Cloud Firestore. When the backend is not configured, the site operates in local demonstration mode and does not create a shared account.

Deletion

The initial release ties authorship to the authenticated Firebase user ID. Full self-service account/content deletion and moderation controls should be added before operating the service as a production community at scale.

Third-party services

Authentication and database functionality may use Google Firebase services and are therefore also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

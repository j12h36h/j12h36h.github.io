Privacy Policy

Last updated August 28, 2026.

Google sign-in

LCS uses Firebase Authentication with Google sign-in. Google and Firebase process the provider information needed to authenticate the account. LCS never receives your Google password.

Your public LCS identity is separate

Your public LCS profile uses the display name you choose. It does not need to match your Google account name or legal name. Your Google email address and Google account name are not written into the public profile document or public posts by the LCS frontend.

Public profile information

The public profile may contain your chosen display name, optional public bio, and a Google profile-picture URL only if you explicitly enable public use of that picture. Profile-picture sharing is off by default.

Public content

Posts, comments, communities, channels, ideas, problems, projects, reactions, follows, and relationships created through the public network are designed to be visible to other visitors. Do not publish private or sensitive information into public content.

Service data

Public network data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Authentication state is handled by Firebase Authentication. The browser may store Firebase authentication state so your sign-in can survive a page refresh.

Provider information shown on Account

Your own Account page may display the Google account name and email returned by Firebase Authentication so you can verify which account is connected. Those values are private UI information and are not used as your public LCS identity unless you independently choose the same display name.

Third-party services

Authentication and database functionality use Google Firebase services and are also subject to Google's applicable privacy practices and terms.

Contact

For LCS project/privacy questions, use the public contact methods available from the project owner's GitHub profile.

Logical Communication Service — Communities + Channels v0.4
Date: 2026-08-28

This update replaces the previous single-level Spaces UI with a functional two-level organization model while preserving the existing `spaces` Firestore collection as the community record for backward compatibility.

Implemented:
- Communities directory view.
- Real Firestore-backed `channels` collection.
- New community creation automatically creates #general.
- Community owners can add channels.
- Channel types: discussion, ideas, problems, projects, research, releases, announcements.
- Sidebar community/channel tree.
- Feed, catalogs, and idea map can filter by community or exact channel.
- Post and object records now store both `spaceId` (community) and `channelId`.
- Composer and object creator post directly to a channel.
- Search includes communities and channels.
- Existing pre-v0.4 communities remain usable through a compatibility #general channel until a real general channel exists.

Firebase action required:
Publish the included logicalcommunicationservice/firestore.rules again. The v0.4 rules authorize the new channels collection and channelId fields.
